# Research watch

A weekly scan of new network and graph research (arXiv and network-science, statistics and algorithms journals) and of new releases of the tools surveyed here. Papers are scored by topic and flagged when they propose a method, release code, or report a pitfall in common practice. The scan is automated and unreviewed: a listing is a pointer, not an endorsement. Sources, topics and weights are in [`watch/config.json`](https://github.com/knapply/net-oracles/blob/main/watch/config.json).
