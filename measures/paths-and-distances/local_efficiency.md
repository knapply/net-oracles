# Local efficiency {#concept-top}

Family: [Paths and distances](index.html). Subfamily: Efficiency and reachability. Concept id: `local_efficiency`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\); every edge has a source and a target</td></tr><tr><td>\(N(v)\)</td><td>neighbours of \(v\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(G[N(v)]\)</td><td>subgraph induced by the neighbours of \(v\) (the node itself excluded in the definition)</td></tr><tr><td>\(E_{\mathrm{glob}}\)</td><td>global efficiency, the mean of \(1/d\) over ordered pairs of distinct nodes</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Mean over the nodes of the global efficiency of the subgraph induced by each node's neighbors.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Local efficiency (Latora and Marchiori 2001)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[E_{\mathrm{loc}}(G)=\frac1n\sum_{v\in V}E_{\mathrm{glob}}\bigl(G[N(v)]\bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean over nodes of the global efficiency of the subgraph induced by each node's neighbours, with \(v\) itself removed. A neighbourhood of fewer than two nodes has \(n(n-1)=0\) pairs, taken as 0.</p></div>
