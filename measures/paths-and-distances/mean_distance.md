# Mean distance {#concept-top}

Family: [Paths and distances](index.html). Subfamily: Distance summaries. Concept id: `mean_distance`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\); every edge has a source and a target</td></tr><tr><td>\(d(s,t)\)</td><td>length of a shortest path from \(s\) to \(t\): the number of edges, or the total of the edge lengths for a weighted graph; \(d(s,s)=0\); \(\infty\) if \(t\) cannot be reached from \(s\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(P\)</td><td>ordered pairs \((s,t)\), \(s\ne t\), with \(d(s,t)&lt;\infty\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Mean shortest-path distance over ordered pairs of distinct nodes joined by a path.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Mean distance (characteristic path length, Watts and Strogatz 1998)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\bar d=\frac{1}{|P|}\sum_{(s,t)\in P}d(s,t)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean shortest-path distance over ordered pairs of distinct nodes joined by a path; defined when \(P\ne\emptyset\), i.e. when at least one edge connects two distinct nodes. For a connected graph \(P\) is all \(n(n-1)\) ordered pairs.</p></div>
