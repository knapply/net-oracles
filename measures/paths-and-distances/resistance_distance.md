# Resistance distance and effective resistance {#concept-top}

Family: [Paths and distances](index.html). Subfamily: Resistance and spectral distances. Concept id: `resistance_distance`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Resistance distance between every pair of nodes, infinite across components (Klein and Randic 1993).

*Definition partly written:* resistance\_based (NetworKit's resistance-based and spectral edge and vertex scores) is documented only and was never run; its definition is not recorded in the sources.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Resistance distance (Klein and Randic 1993)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Omega_{ij}=L^{+}_{ii}+L^{+}_{jj}-2L^{+}_{ij}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Effective resistance between two nodes when each edge is a unit resistor (or a conductor of the given weight). Infinite between components.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Effective graph resistance, Kirchhoff index (Ellens et al. 2011)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[R_G=\sum_{i&lt;j}\Omega_{ij}=n\,\mathrm{tr}(L^{+})\]</div><p class="formula-where">Sum of resistance distances over all pairs; infinite for a disconnected graph.</p></div>
