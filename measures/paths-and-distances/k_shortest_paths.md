# K shortest paths {#concept-top}

Family: [Paths and distances](index.html). Subfamily: Shortest paths. Concept id: `k_shortest_paths`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\); every edge has a source and a target</td></tr><tr><td>\(\mathcal P^{\mathrm{loopless}}_{st}\)</td><td>loopless (simple) paths from \(s\) to \(t\), ordered by length</td></tr><tr><td>\(k\)</td><td>number of paths requested</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

K shortest paths. Entries grouped here: Paths k shortest.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>k shortest loopless paths (Yen 1971)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: non-negative</span></p><div class="formula">\[\pi_1,\dots,\pi_k\ \text{with}\ \ell(\pi_1)\le\dots\le\ell(\pi_k)\le\ell(\pi)\ \ \forall\pi\in\mathcal P_{st}\setminus\{\pi_1..\pi_k\}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The \(k\) shortest simple paths in order of length; fewer when the graph has fewer than \(k\) simple paths. With ties the lengths are determined but not which of several equal-length paths is the \(i\)-th.</p></div>
