## Definition {#concept-definition}

Force-directed layouts. Entries grouped here: Davidson-Harel layout; DrL (OpenOrd) layout; Fruchterman-Reingold layout; GEM layout; Graphopt layout; Gursoy atun layout; and 4 more.

*Definition partly written:* The Fruchterman-Reingold forces and the Kamada-Kawai energy are written from their papers. Davidson-Harel, DrL/OpenOrd, GEM, graphopt, LGL, multilevel, spring and the Gursoy-Atun layouts are named by the sources only; their variants have no entry.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Fruchterman-Reingold forces (Fruchterman and Reingold 1991)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[f_a(d)=\frac{d^2}{k}\ \text{(between adjacent nodes)},\qquad f_r(d)=-\frac{k^2}{d}\ \text{(between all pairs)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Attraction along edges and repulsion between every pair; each iteration moves a node along the sum of its forces by at most \(t\), and \(t\) is cooled. The result depends on the initial (usually random) positions, so a seed fixes it.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Kamada-Kawai energy (Kamada and Kawai 1989)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any, connected</span></p><div class="formula">\[E(X)=\sum_{i&lt;j}\frac{K}{2\,d_{ij}^{2}}\bigl(\|x_i-x_j\|-L\,d_{ij}\bigr)^2\]</div><p class="formula-where">Spring system with natural length proportional to the graph distance and spring constant inversely proportional to its square; defined for connected graphs (\(d_{ij}&lt;\infty\)).</p></div>
