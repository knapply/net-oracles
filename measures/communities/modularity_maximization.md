## Definition {#concept-definition}

A partition of maximum modularity found exactly by Russian doll search.

*Definition partly written:* cat\_hierarchy\_louvain and cat\_iteration\_louvain (scikit-network's Louvain hierarchy and iteration) are documented only and were never run; what levels or iterations they return is not recorded in the sources.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Objective</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\max_{\mathcal P}Q(\mathcal P)\]</div><p class="formula-where">All procedures below search for a partition of high modularity \(Q\) (defined in the modularity concept); maximizing it is NP-hard (Brandes et al. 2008), so most procedures are heuristics, and the exact search is feasible only on small graphs.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Louvain move gain (Blondel et al. 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Delta Q=\Bigl[\frac{\Sigma_{\mathrm{in}}+2k_{i,\mathrm{in}}}{2m}-\Bigl(\frac{\Sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}+k_i}{2m}\Bigr)^2\Bigr]-\Bigl[\frac{\Sigma_{\mathrm{in}}}{2m}-\Bigl(\frac{\Sigma_{\mathrm{tot}}}{2m}\Bigr)^2-\Bigl(\frac{k_i}{2m}\Bigr)^2\Bigr]\]</div><p class="formula-where">Gain of moving the isolated node \(i\) into community \(C\); nodes are moved while the gain is positive, then communities are aggregated into nodes and the step repeated. The visiting order and tie handling are not fixed by the definition, so results depend on them. Leiden (Traag et al. 2019) adds a refinement that keeps communities connected and well separated.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Greedy agglomeration (Clauset, Newman and Moore 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Delta Q_{ab}=2\,(e_{ab}-a_aa_b)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Repeatedly merge the pair of communities with the largest \(\Delta Q\) (adjacent pairs only); the best level of the dendrogram is the maximum of \(Q\) along the merge sequence.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Leading eigenvector (Newman 2006)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Delta Q=\frac{1}{4m}\,s^{\mathsf T}Bs,\qquad s=\mathrm{sign}(u_1)\]</div><p class="formula-where">A group is split by the signs of the leading eigenvector \(u_1\) of its (generalized) modularity matrix when the leading eigenvalue is positive; repeated until no split raises \(Q\). Zero components (isolated nodes) and repeated eigenvalues leave the split undetermined.</p></div>
