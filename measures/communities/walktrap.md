# Walktrap {#concept-top}

Family: [Communities](index.html). Subfamily: Other detection algorithms. Concept id: `walktrap`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Walktrap communities by merging nodes whose random-walk distributions are close; returns the best level, its modularity, the mergers and the modularity path.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Walktrap distance (Pons and Latapy 2006)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[r_{ij}^2=\sum_{k}\frac{(P^t_{ik}-P^t_{jk})^2}{d(k)},\qquad r^2_{C_1C_2}=\sum_k\frac{(P^t_{C_1k}-P^t_{C_2k})^2}{d(k)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Distance between the walk distributions of two nodes or communities; \(P^t_{Ck}\) is the average over members of \(C\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Merge criterion</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Delta\sigma(C_1,C_2)=\frac1n\,\frac{|C_1||C_2|}{|C_1|+|C_2|}\,r^2_{C_1C_2}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Agglomeration merges the adjacent pair with the smallest \(\Delta\sigma\); the dendrogram level with the highest modularity is reported.</p></div>
