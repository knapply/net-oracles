# Girvan-Newman edge removal {#concept-top}

Family: [Communities](index.html). Subfamily: Other detection algorithms. Concept id: `girvan_newman`. 5 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\); every edge has a source and a target (the ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(B(e)\)</td><td>betweenness of edge \(e\): \(\sum_{s\ne t}\sigma_{st}(e)/\sigma_{st}\), the share of shortest paths through it (Brandes 2001)</td></tr><tr><td>\(B_{\mathrm{cf}}(e)\)</td><td>current-flow edge betweenness (Brandes and Fleischer 2005)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Girvan-Newman clustering by removing the edge of highest betweenness one at a time; returns the best level, its modularity, the removal order and the modularity path.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Girvan-Newman procedure (Girvan and Newman 2002)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\text{repeat: remove }e^\star=\arg\max_{e}B(e)\text{ (recomputed after every removal)};\ \text{communities}=\text{components}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Edges between communities carry many shortest paths and are removed first; the removal sequence defines a dendrogram. The level of the dendrogram with highest modularity (Newman and Girvan 2004) or a requested number of groups is reported. Edges of equal betweenness are removed in an order the definition does not fix.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Current-flow variant</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[e^\star=\arg\max_eB_{\mathrm{cf}}(e)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Edge betweenness replaced by current-flow betweenness; removal repeats until at least \(k\) components exist.</p></div>
