## Definition {#concept-definition}

One-mode projection of a two-mode network onto either mode, with count, Newman or product weights.

*Definition partly written:* Count, Newman and sum-of-products weights are written from their papers. The overlap, Jaccard, covariance, correlation, Bonacich and cosine weights of the measures projection and projection\_weights are named by the tools but their formulas are not given here; variants are given only for the measure project.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Shared-neighbour count (Breiger 1974)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite, two_mode</span></p><div class="formula">\[P_{uv}=\sum_{e}B_{ue}B_{ve}=(BB^{\top})_{uv}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The number of nodes of the dropped class adjacent to both \(u\) and \(v\). Defined for \(u\ne v\); the diagonal of \(BB^{\top}\) is the degree.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Sum of products</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite, weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[P_{uv}=\sum_{e}w_{ue}\,w_{ve}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The weighted form of the count; with unit weights it equals the count.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Newman collaboration weights (Newman 2001)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite, two_mode</span></p><div class="formula">\[P_{uv}=\sum_{e:\,k_e\ge2}\frac{B_{ue}B_{ve}}{k_e-1}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Each shared node \(e\) contributes \(1/(k_e-1)\), so a large group dilutes the tie. Nodes with \(k_e=1\) join no pair and are skipped (the term would be 0/0).</p></div>
