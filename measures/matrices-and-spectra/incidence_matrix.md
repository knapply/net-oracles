# Incidence matrix {#concept-top}

Family: [Matrices and spectra](index.html). Subfamily: Graph matrices. Concept id: `incidence_matrix`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Incidence matrix. Entries grouped here: Incidence.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Unoriented incidence matrix (Bondy and Murty 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[b_{ve}=\begin{cases}1&amp;v\text{ is an end of }e\\0&amp;\text{otherwise}\end{cases}\]</div><p class="formula-where">One column per edge, with 1 at both ends; a self-loop is a column with no entry in the convention that loops are empty (the other convention puts 2 at the loop's node).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Oriented incidence matrix</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[b_{ve}=\begin{cases}-1&amp;v=s(e)\\+1&amp;v=t(e)\\0&amp;\text{otherwise}\end{cases}\]</div><p class="formula-where">\(-1\) at the source and \(+1\) at the target of each edge; \(BB^{\top}=D-A_{\text{sym}}\) is the combinatorial Laplacian of the underlying undirected graph.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Biadjacency matrix</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite</span></p><div class="formula">\[m_{pq}=\text{number (or total weight) of edges between }p\in P\text{ and }q\in Q\]</div><p class="formula-where">The part of the adjacency matrix of a bipartite graph that joins the two classes; it has \(|P|\) rows and \(|Q|\) columns, so it is not square in general.</p></div>
