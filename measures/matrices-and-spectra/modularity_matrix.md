# Modularity matrix {#concept-top}

Family: [Matrices and spectra](index.html). Subfamily: Graph matrices. Concept id: `modularity_matrix`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(m=|E|\)</td><td>number of edges</td></tr><tr><td>\(A=(a_{uv})\)</td><td>adjacency matrix: \(a_{uv}\) is the number of edges from \(u\) to \(v\) (the sum of their weights when weighted)</td></tr><tr><td>\(k_u\)</td><td>degree of \(u\); \(k_u^{\text{out}},k_u^{\text{in}}\) out- and in-degree</td></tr><tr><td>\(m\)</td><td>number of edges (total weight)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Modularity matrix. Entries grouped here: Gm modularity.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Modularity matrix (Newman 2006)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[B_{uv}=a_{uv}-\frac{k_uk_v}{2m}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Observed minus expected tie weight under the configuration null model; needs \(m&gt;0\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Directed modularity matrix (Leicht and Newman 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[B_{uv}=a_{uv}-\frac{k_u^{\text{out}}k_v^{\text{in}}}{m}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Out-degree of the source times in-degree of the target, divided by \(m\).</p></div>
