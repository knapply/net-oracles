# Adjacency matrix {#concept-top}

Family: [Matrices and spectra](index.html). Subfamily: Graph matrices. Concept id: `adjacency_matrix`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(m=|E|\)</td><td>number of edges</td></tr><tr><td>\(w(e)\)</td><td>weight of edge \(e\) (1 when unweighted)</td></tr><tr><td>\(s(e),t(e)\)</td><td>source and target of edge \(e\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Adjacency matrix. Entries grouped here: Adjacency.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Adjacency matrix (Harary 1969; Biggs 1974)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[a_{uv}=\sum_{e:\,s(e)=u,\ t(e)=v}w(e)\qquad\text{and, if undirected, also }a_{vu}=a_{uv}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Entry \((u,v)\) counts the edges from \(u\) to \(v\) (sums their weights). An undirected edge fills both \((u,v)\) and \((v,u)\); the convention for an undirected self-loop is that it adds its weight once to \(a_{uu}\) (a loop contributes 1 to the degree under the convention \(d_u=\sum_v a_{uv}\); the other convention adds 2).</p></div>
