## Definition {#concept-definition}

Pairwise structural equivalence of all nodes: Euclidean distance, correlation or Hamming distance between their tie profiles.

*Definition partly written:* cat\_correlation\_diagonal\_distance\_equivalence\_etc\_euclidean\_hamming\_jaccard\_manhattan\_matches\_measures\_options\_profile\_row\_similarities\_structural and equivalence\_extra (UCINET: profile similarities and distances such as Jaccard, matches, Manhattan, overlaps, cross-products and coverage) are documented only and never run; their formulas are not recorded in the sources.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Structural equivalence (Lorrain and White 1971)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[i\sim j\iff x_{ik}=x_{jk}\ \text{and}\ x_{ki}=x_{kj}\ \ \forall k\in K_{ij}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Two nodes are structurally equivalent when they have identical ties to every other node (and, for directed ties, identical incoming ties).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Euclidean distance between profiles</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[d_{ij}=\sqrt{\sum_{k\in K_{ij}}\bigl[(x_{ik}-x_{jk})^2+(x_{ki}-x_{kj})^2\bigr]}\]</div><p class="formula-where">0 exactly for structurally equivalent nodes; row and column profiles are both used for directed data.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Correlation of profiles (Burt 1976)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[r_{ij}=\mathrm{corr}\bigl((x_{ik},x_{ki})_{k\in K_{ij}},(x_{jk},x_{kj})_{k\in K_{ij}}\bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Pearson correlation between the stacked row and column profiles; 0/0 when a profile is constant.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Hamming distance</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[h_{ij}=\sum_{k\in K_{ij}}\bigl[\mathbb 1(x_{ik}\ne x_{jk})+\mathbb 1(x_{ki}\ne x_{kj})\bigr]\]</div><p class="formula-where">Number of disagreeing entries.</p></div>
