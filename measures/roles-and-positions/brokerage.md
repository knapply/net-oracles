## Definition {#concept-definition}

Brokerage. Entries grouped here: Brokerage.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Brokerage roles (Gould and Fernandez 1989)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[w_{R}(j)=\bigl|\{(i,j,k)\in T_j: \text{labels of }(g(i),g(j),g(k))\text{ match role }R\}\bigr|\]</div><p class="formula-where">The broker \(j\) mediates a tie from \(i\) to \(k\) that has no direct tie. Roles: coordinator (\(g(i)=g(j)=g(k)\)), itinerant broker (\(g(i)=g(k)\ne g(j)\)), gatekeeper (\(g(j)=g(k)\ne g(i)\)), representative (\(g(i)=g(j)\ne g(k)\)), liaison (all three different); the total is \(\sum_R w_R(j)=|T_j|\). Counts are defined for every node and are 0 when no two-path exists.</p></div>
