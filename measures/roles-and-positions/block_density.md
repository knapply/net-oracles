## Definition {#concept-definition}

Block density. Entries grouped here: Block density.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Block density (White, Boorman and Breiger 1976)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[d_{rs}=\frac{\sum_{i\in B_r}\sum_{j\in B_s,\,j\ne i}x_{ij}}{n_rn_s-\mathbb 1[r=s]\,n_r}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean tie value from block \(r\) to block \(s\) over the possible pairs of distinct nodes (a binary \(x\) gives the proportion of present ties). Defined when the block pair has at least one possible pair: not for a block of one node on the diagonal (\(n_r^2-n_r=0\)).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Block density counting loops</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[d^{\circ}_{rr}=\frac{\sum_{i,j\in B_r}x_{ij}}{n_r^2}\]</div><p class="formula-where">When loops are possible ties the diagonal pairs are included; defined for \(n_r\ge1\).</p></div>
