# Clique number {#concept-top}

Family: [Cohesion and subgroups](index.html). Subfamily: Cliques. Concept id: `clique_number`. 2 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>simple undirected graph (direction ignored, parallel edges merged, loops dropped) with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(\mathcal C(G)\)</td><td>set of cliques: node sets \(S\) in which every two distinct nodes are adjacent</td></tr><tr><td>\(\omega(v)\)</td><td>size of the largest clique containing \(v\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Size of the largest clique.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Clique number</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[\omega(G)=\max_{S\in\mathcal C(G)}|S|\]</div><p class="formula-where">Size of a largest clique; 0 for the empty graph, at least 1 otherwise. Maximum cliques are found among the maximal cliques of Bron and Kerbosch 1973 (worst-case bound by Tomita et al. 2006, listing in degeneracy order by Eppstein et al. 2010).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Node clique number</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[\omega(v)=\max\{|S|:S\in\mathcal C(G),\,v\in S\}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Largest clique containing the node; at least 1 for every node.</p></div>
