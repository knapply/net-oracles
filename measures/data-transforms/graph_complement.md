## Definition {#concept-definition}

Graph complement. Entries grouped here: Graph complement (graph\_complement); Graph complement (op\_complement).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Complement (Harary 1969)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[V(\overline G)=V,\qquad E(\overline G)=\{uv:\ u\neq v,\ uv\notin E\}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Two distinct nodes are adjacent in \(\overline G\) exactly when they are not adjacent in \(G\). For a digraph the pair is ordered: \((u,v)\in E(\overline G)\) exactly when \((u,v)\notin E\), \(u\neq v\). Defined for simple graphs; edge multiplicity and weights have no complement.</p></div>
