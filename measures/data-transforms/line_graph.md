# Line graph {#concept-top}

Family: [Data transforms](index.html). Subfamily: Graph operators. Concept id: `line_graph`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(m=|E|\)</td><td>number of edges</td></tr><tr><td>\(L(G)\)</td><td>line graph of \(G\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(s(e),t(e)\)</td><td>source and target of edge \(e\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Line graph. Entries grouped here: Line graph.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Line graph (Harary 1969)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[V(L(G))=E,\qquad ef\in E(L(G))\iff e\neq f\ \text{and}\ e,f\ \text{share an end}\]</div><p class="formula-where">One node per edge of \(G\); two are adjacent when the edges meet at a node. \(|V(L(G))|=m\), and node \(e=uv\) has degree \(d_u+d_v-2\) in a simple graph.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Directed line graph</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[(e,f)\in E(L(G))\iff t(e)=s(f)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Arc \(e\) leads to arc \(f\) when the target of \(e\) is the source of \(f\); a walk in \(L(G)\) is a walk of arcs of \(G\).</p></div>
