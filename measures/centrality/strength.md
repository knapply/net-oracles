# Strength (weighted degree) {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Degree and strength. Concept id: `strength`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(w(u,v)\)</td><td>weight of the edge from \(u\) to \(v\) (1 when the graph is unweighted)</td></tr><tr><td>\(s(v)\)</td><td>strength of \(v\): total weight of the edges at \(v\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(s^{\mathrm{in}}(v),\,s^{\mathrm{out}}(v)\)</td><td>total weight of the edges with target \(v\) and with source \(v\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Strength (weighted degree). Entries grouped here: Strength.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Strength (Barrat et al. 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[s(v)=\sum_{u}w(v,u)\quad\bigl(+\,w(v,v)\ \text{for an undirected loop}\bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Sum of the weights of all edge ends at \(v\) (parallel edges add; an undirected loop is counted at both ends). With unit weights it equals the degree.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>In- and out-strength</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[s^{\mathrm{in}}(v)=\sum_{u}w(u,v),\qquad s^{\mathrm{out}}(v)=\sum_{u}w(v,u)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Directed analogues.</p></div>
