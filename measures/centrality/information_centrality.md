# Information centrality (current-flow closeness) {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Resistance and information. Concept id: `information_centrality`. 2 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(A^*\)</td><td>matrix with \(A^*_{ii}=1+\sum_j w_{ij}\) and \(A^*_{ij}=1-w_{ij}\) for \(i\ne j\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(C=(A^*)^{-1}\)</td><td>its inverse</td></tr><tr><td>\(T=\operatorname{tr}C,\ R_i=\sum_jC_{ij}\)</td><td>trace and row sums of \(C\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(R_{uv}\)</td><td>effective resistance between \(u\) and \(v\) (resistance distance, edge weights as conductances)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Stephenson and Zelen's information centrality of every node.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Information centrality (Stephenson and Zelen 1989)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, connected, weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[I_i=\frac{1}{C_{ii}+(T-2R_i)/n}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the mean of the information-based distances to all nodes. Defined for connected undirected graphs; \(A^*\) is non-singular for connected graphs.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Current-flow closeness (Brandes and Fleischer 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, connected</span></p><div class="formula">\[c_{CC}(v)=\frac{n-1}{\sum_{u\ne v}R_{vu}}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the mean resistance distance; equal to Stephenson and Zelen's index up to scale.</p></div>
