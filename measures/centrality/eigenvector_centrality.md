# Eigenvector centrality {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Spectral and walk-based. Concept id: `eigenvector_centrality`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Eigenvector centrality of every node, with the principal eigenvalue and a connectedness flag.
