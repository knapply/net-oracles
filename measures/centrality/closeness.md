Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Distance-based. Concept id: `closeness`. 8 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Closeness of every node: the inverse of its mean distance to the nodes it reaches, with the unreachable-node rule of the chosen tool.

*Definition partly written:* Closeness assembled in netrankr (cat\_closeness\_swan) and the weighted closeness of rustworkx are described from the tools' documentation names only; their formulas are not stated in the sources read.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Closeness (Sabidussi 1966; Freeman 1978)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, raw</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C(v)=\frac{1}{\sum_{u\ne v}d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the sum of distances to all other nodes; defined when every other node is reachable. Direction: distances out of \(v\) (out-closeness) or into \(v\) (in-closeness).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Normalized closeness</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: connected, normalized</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C'(v)=\frac{n-1}{\sum_{u\ne v}d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the mean distance; 1 when \(v\) is adjacent to every other node. Needs \(n\ge 2\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Reachable-nodes reading</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: normalized, directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C^{R}(v)=\frac{r_v}{\sum_{u\in R(v)}d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the mean distance to the nodes that \(v\) reaches; undefined (0/0) when \(r_v=0\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Improved closeness (Wasserman and Faust 1994)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: normalized, directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C^{WF}(v)=\frac{r_v}{n-1}\cdot\frac{r_v}{\sum_{u\in R(v)}d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The reachable-nodes reading scaled by the fraction of nodes reached, so nodes that reach little score low; 0 when \(r_v=0\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Residual closeness (Dangalchev 2006)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C^{\mathrm{res}}(v)=\sum_{u\ne v}2^{-d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Nearby nodes dominate; unreachable nodes contribute 0.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Two-mode closeness (Borgatti and Everett 1997)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: two_mode, normalized</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C^{(2)}(v)=\frac{n_{\text{other}}+2(n_{\text{own}}-1)}{\sum_{u\ne v}d(v,u)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Normalized by the smallest possible distance sum of a node in a mode of size \(n_{\text{own}}\) with \(n_{\text{other}}\) nodes in the other mode: all other-mode nodes at distance 1 and the rest of its own mode at distance 2.</p></div>
