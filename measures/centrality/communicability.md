# Communicability {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Spectral and walk-based. Concept id: `communicability`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(A\)</td><td>adjacency matrix: \(A_{uv}\) is the number (or total weight) of edges from \(u\) to \(v\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(G_{pq}\)</td><td>communicability between \(p\) and \(q\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Communicability of every pair of nodes: the matrix exponential of the adjacency matrix, per component (Estrada and Hatano 2008).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Communicability (Estrada and Hatano 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[G_{pq}=\bigl(e^{A}\bigr)_{pq}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(A^k)_{pq}}{k!}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Walks of every length between \(p\) and \(q\), weighted by \(1/k!\) so short walks dominate; with \(p=q\) it is the subgraph centrality of \(p\).</p></div>
