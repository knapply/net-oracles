# Current-flow betweenness {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Betweenness, load and flow. Concept id: `current_flow_betweenness`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Current-flow betweenness approximated from sampled pairs of nodes (random pairs or given ones).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Throughput of a node (Newman 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, connected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\tau_{st}(i)=\tfrac12\sum_{j}A_{ij}\,\bigl|V^{(st)}_i-V^{(st)}_j\bigr|\quad (i\ne s,t),\qquad \tau_{st}(s)=\tau_{st}(t)=1\]</div><p class="formula-where">Half the total absolute current on the edges at \(i\) when a unit current is driven from \(s\) to \(t\) (Kirchhoff's laws, unit resistances; weights are conductances).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Current-flow betweenness (Brandes and Fleischer 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, connected, normalized</span></p><div class="formula">\[c_B(v)=\frac{1}{(n-1)(n-2)}\sum_{\substack{s\ne t\\ s,t\ne v}}\tau_{st}(v)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean throughput over pairs of other nodes. The edge version replaces \(\tau_{st}(v)\) by the absolute current \(|V^{(st)}_u-V^{(st)}_w|\) on edge \((u,w)\) with a normalizer \(n(n-1)/2\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Approximation by sampled pairs</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, connected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\hat c_B(v)=\frac{1}{|P|}\sum_{(s,t)\in P}\tau_{st}(v)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Average over a random sample \(P\) of pairs (Brandes and Fleischer 2005); an unbiased estimate of the mean over all pairs.</p></div>
