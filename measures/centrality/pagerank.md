# PageRank {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Spectral and walk-based. Concept id: `pagerank`. 5 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(P\)</td><td>row-stochastic transition matrix: \(P_{uv}=A_{uv}/\sum_wA_{uw}\) for nodes with out-edges</td></tr><tr><td>\(d\)</td><td>damping factor (0.85 by default)</td></tr><tr><td>\(v\)</td><td>personalization (teleport) distribution, uniform by default</td></tr><tr><td>\(u\)</td><td>distribution that a node without out-edges (dangling) jumps by, uniform by default</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

PageRank of every node by power iteration (Brin and Page 1998), with a GAP-suite preset.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>PageRank (Brin and Page 1998)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any, weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[\pi=d\Bigl(P^{\mathsf T}\pi+\bigl(\textstyle\sum_{w\in D}\pi_w\bigr)\,u\Bigr)+(1-d)\,v,\qquad \textstyle\sum_i\pi_i=1\]</div><p class="formula-where">Stationary distribution of a random surfer who follows an out-edge with probability \(d\) and teleports with probability \(1-d\); \(D\) is the set of dangling nodes. Unique for \(0\le d&lt;1\); computed by power iteration or a linear system.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Personalized PageRank</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\pi=d\Bigl(P^{\mathsf T}\pi+\bigl(\textstyle\sum_{w\in D}\pi_w\bigr)u\Bigr)+(1-d)\,v,\quad v=\text{seed distribution}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The same with a non-uniform teleport distribution \(v\); the local push method of Andersen, Chung and Lang (2006) approximates it from below by at most the residual.</p></div>
