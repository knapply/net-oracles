# Load centrality {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Betweenness, load and flow. Concept id: `load_centrality`. 2 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(\mathrm{succ}_t(u)\)</td><td>neighbours \(w\) of \(u\) with \(d(u,w)+d(w,t)=d(u,t)\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(f_{st}(v)\)</td><td>share of the unit sent from \(s\) to \(t\) that reaches \(v\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Load centrality of every node: flow split equally among predecessors (Goh, Kahng and Kim 2001).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Load (Goh, Kahng and Kim 2001)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any, raw</span></p><div class="formula">\[\ell(v)=\sum_{s\ne v}\ \sum_{t\ne s,v} f_{st}(v),\qquad f_{st}(s)=1,\ \ f_{st}(w)=\sum_{u:\,w\in\mathrm{succ}_t(u)}\frac{f_{st}(u)}{|\mathrm{succ}_t(u)|}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Each node sends one unit to every other node; at every node the flow is split equally among the next nodes on shortest paths. Equal to betweenness when the shortest-path graph is a tree or has no branching that rejoins.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Normalized load</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: normalized, directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[\ell'(v)=\frac{\ell(v)}{(n-1)(n-2)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Divides by the number of ordered pairs of other nodes; for undirected graphs the sum over unordered pairs is divided by \((n-1)(n-2)/2\).</p></div>
