# Reach and m-reach {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Distance-based. Concept id: `reach_centrality`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Number (or share) of other nodes within distance k of every node.

*Definition partly written:* The formula of M-reach closeness (cat\_closeness\_mreach) is not recorded in the sources read.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Reach (m-reach)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\mathrm{reach}_k(v)=\bigl|B_k(v)\bigr|,\qquad \mathrm{reach}_k'(v)=\frac{|B_k(v)|}{n-1}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Number, or share, of the other nodes within \(k\) steps (Borgatti and Everett 2006 call the unrestricted case reach; with \(k=1\) it is degree for simple graphs). Direction: out- or in-reach.</p></div>
