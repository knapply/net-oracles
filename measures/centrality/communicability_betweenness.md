# Communicability betweenness {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Betweenness, load and flow. Concept id: `communicability_betweenness`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Communicability betweenness of every node: the loss of communicability when the node's edges are removed (Estrada, Higham and Hatano 2009).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Communicability betweenness (Estrada, Higham and Hatano 2009)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, normalized</span></p><div class="formula">\[\omega_r=\frac{1}{(n-1)^2-(n-1)}\sum_{p}\sum_{q}\frac{G_{pq}-G_{pq}(r)}{G_{pq}},\qquad p\ne q,\ p\ne r,\ q\ne r\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean relative loss of communicability between other pairs when the edges at \(r\) are deleted. The terms need \(G_{pq}&gt;0\) (a walk between \(p\) and \(q\)). The factor \((n-1)^2-(n-1)\) is zero for \(n\le 2\).</p></div>
