# Group closeness centrality {#concept-top}

Family: [Centrality](index.html). Subfamily: Distance-based. Concept id: `group_closeness`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(S\subset V\)</td><td>the group</td></tr><tr><td>\(d(S,v)\)</td><td>distance from the group to \(v\): \(\min_{u\in S}d(u,v)\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Group degree, closeness or betweenness of a set of nodes (Everett and Borgatti).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Group closeness (Everett and Borgatti 1999)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: group, normalized</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_C(S)=\frac{n-|S|}{\sum_{v\notin S}d(S,v)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Inverse of the mean distance from the group to the non-members; 1 when every non-member is adjacent to a member. The raw form is \(1/\sum_{v\notin S}d(S,v)\).</p></div>
