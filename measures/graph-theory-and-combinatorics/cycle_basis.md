## Definition {#concept-definition}

Cycle basis. Entries grouped here: Cycle basis; Basis; Nxs cycle basis.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Fundamental cycle basis (Paton 1969; Kirchhoff 1847)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\{C_e:\ e\in E\setminus T\},\qquad |\text{basis}|=m-n+c\]</div><p class="formula-where">The cycle space over GF(2) has dimension \(m-n+c\) (cyclomatic number); the cycles closed by the non-tree edges of a spanning forest are a basis. The basis depends on the forest chosen, so the cycles are not unique, only their number.</p></div>
