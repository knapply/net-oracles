# Simple cycles {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Cycles. Concept id: `simple_cycles`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Every simple cycle, by Johnson's search or a bounded depth-first search.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Simple cycles (Johnson 1975)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\mathcal C=\{v_1v_2\cdots v_kv_1:\ v_i\ \text{distinct}\}/\!\sim,\qquad \text{optionally}\ k\le L\]</div><p class="formula-where">Every simple cycle once, by Johnson's search (digraphs) or a bounded depth-first search for undirected graphs. The number of cycles can be exponential, which is why a length bound \(L\) is offered.</p></div>
