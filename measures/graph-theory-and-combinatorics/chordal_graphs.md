## Definition {#concept-definition}

Chordal graphs. Entries grouped here: Chordal order; Chordal-graph tests and algorithms; Fill; St chordal.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Chordal graph (Tarjan and Yannakakis 1984)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[G\ \text{chordal}\iff\text{every cycle of length}\ge4\text{ has a chord}\iff\exists\ \text{perfect elimination ordering}\]</div><p class="formula-where">An ordering in which every node's later neighbours form a clique. Maximum cardinality search produces such an ordering exactly when \(G\) is chordal.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Treewidth of a chordal graph</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\operatorname{tw}(G)=\omega(G)-1\]</div><p class="formula-where">The maximal cliques are the bags of an optimal tree decomposition.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Minimal triangulation (Berry, Blair and Heggernes 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[E(H)=E(G)\cup F,\ H\ \text{chordal},\ \text{no proper subset of the fill edges }F\text{ gives a chordal graph}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Fill edges \(F\) added to make the graph chordal, minimal under inclusion (not minimum in size); not unique.</p></div>
