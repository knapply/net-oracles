# Maximum-weight matching {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Matching. Concept id: `maximum_weight_matching`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

A maximum-weight matching of an undirected network, as input edge positions (blossom algorithm).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Maximum-weight matching (Edmonds 1965; Galil 1986)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[M^{*}=\arg\max_{M\ \text{matching}}\sum_{e\in M}w(e)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Found in polynomial time by the blossom algorithm. With the cardinality option it is the maximum weight among maximum-cardinality matchings, \(\max_{M\in\mathcal M_{\max}}w(M)\).</p></div>
