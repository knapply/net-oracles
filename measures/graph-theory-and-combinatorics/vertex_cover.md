## Definition {#concept-definition}

Local-ratio vertex cover of least node cost (2-approximation), as node ids.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Vertex cover</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[C\subseteq V:\ \forall uv\in E:\ u\in C\ \text{or}\ v\in C;\qquad \tau(G)=\min|C|\]</div><p class="formula-where">Every edge has an end in \(C\); NP-hard in general.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Konig's theorem</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite</span></p><div class="formula">\[\tau(G)=\nu(G)\quad(G\ \text{bipartite})\]</div><p class="formula-where">A minimum vertex cover of a bipartite graph is computed from a maximum matching.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Local-ratio 2-approximation (Bar-Yehuda and Even 1985)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\sum_{v\in C}c(v)\le2\min_{C'}\sum_{v\in C'}c(v)\]</div><p class="formula-where">For each uncovered edge reduce the costs of both ends by the smaller of the two; nodes of cost 0 form the cover.</p></div>
