## Definition {#concept-definition}

Planarity test. Entries grouped here: Planar; Planarity testing, embedding and straight-line drawing.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Planarity (de Fraysseix and Rosenstiehl 1982; Demoucron, Malgrange and Pertuiset 1964)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[G\ \text{planar}\iff G\ \text{can be drawn in the plane with no crossing edges}\iff\text{every biconnected block embeds}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The left-right criterion decides this in linear time (Brandes 2009); the path-addition method of Demoucron et al. embeds block by block.</p></div>
