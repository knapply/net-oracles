# Node contraction {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Spanners, contraction and reduction. Concept id: `contracted_nodes`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Node contraction. Entries grouped here: Contracted nodes.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Node contraction (Bondy and Murty 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[V'=V\setminus\{v\},\qquad E'=\{(\phi(a),\phi(b)):\ (a,b)\in E\},\ \ \phi(v)=u,\ \phi(x)=x\ (x\ne v)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Identify \(v\) with \(u\): every edge at \(v\) is moved to \(u\). Edges between \(u\) and \(v\) become loops at \(u\) (or are dropped), and parallel edges can arise (kept in a multigraph, merged in a simple graph). Edge contraction is the case in which \(u,v\) are adjacent. A quotient graph contracts a whole partition.</p></div>
