## Definition {#concept-definition}

A maximum matching of a bipartite graph as each node's mate (-1 if unmatched), by Hopcroft and Karp.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Maximum bipartite matching (Hopcroft and Karp 1973)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: bipartite</span></p><div class="formula">\[M^{*}=\arg\max_{M\ \text{matching}}|M|,\qquad |M^{*}|=\nu(G)=\tau(G)\ \ (\text{Konig})\]</div><p class="formula-where">Found in \(O(m\sqrt n)\) by alternating breadth-first search and depth-first augmentation. The size is unique; the matching is not, unless forced. The set of matched nodes also depends on the matching.</p></div>
