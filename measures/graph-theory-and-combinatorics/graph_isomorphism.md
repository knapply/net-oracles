## Definition {#concept-definition}

Graph isomorphism. Entries grouped here: Could be iso; Di Graph Matcher; Graph Matcher; Match vertices; Multi Di Graph Matcher; Multi Graph Matcher; and 1 more.

*Definition partly written:* The documented matcher entries (cat\_\*) are known by name only; the invariants of could\_be\_iso are listed without a cited primary source.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Graph isomorphism (Cordella et al. 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[G_1\cong G_2\iff\exists\,\varphi\ \text{bijective}:\ uv\in E_1\iff\varphi(u)\varphi(v)\in E_2\]</div><p class="formula-where">For digraphs ordered pairs; for multigraphs the multiplicities must agree; with colours the colours must agree. VF2 searches with feasibility rules, VF2++ (Juttner and Madarasi 2018) orders the nodes first so that the search prunes earlier.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Necessary conditions ('could be isomorphic')</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[G_1\cong G_2\Rightarrow\ \text{same }n,\ \text{same degree sequence},\ \text{same triangle counts},\ \text{same clique counts}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Cheap invariants that can prove non-isomorphism but never isomorphism.</p></div>
