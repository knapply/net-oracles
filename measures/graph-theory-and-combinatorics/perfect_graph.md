# Perfect graphs {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Chordal, perfect and special graph classes. Concept id: `perfect_graph`. 2 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Perfect graphs. Entries grouped here: Perfect-graph (Berge) tests; Perfect.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Perfect graph (Berge); Strong Perfect Graph Theorem (Chudnovsky et al. 2006)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\chi(H)=\omega(H)\ \ \forall\ \text{induced }H\subseteq G\iff G\ \text{has no odd hole or odd antihole of length}\ge5\]</div><p class="formula-where">An odd hole is an induced cycle of odd length\(\ge5\); an odd antihole is the complement of one. Testing it takes polynomial time (the same paper), but the definition by induced subgraphs is exponential.</p></div>
