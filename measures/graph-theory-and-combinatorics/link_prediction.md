## Definition {#concept-definition}

Link prediction score of each listed pair of nodes: common neighbors, Jaccard, Adamic-Adar and the other neighborhood indices.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Common neighbours (Liben-Nowell and Kleinberg 2007)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{\mathrm{CN}}(u,v)=|\mathrm{CN}(u,v)|\]</div><p class="formula-where">Number of shared neighbours.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Jaccard coefficient</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{J}(u,v)=\frac{|\mathrm{CN}(u,v)|}{|\Gamma(u)\cup\Gamma(v)|}\]</div><p class="formula-where">\(0/0\) when both nodes are isolated.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Adamic-Adar index (Adamic and Adar 2003)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{AA}(u,v)=\sum_{w\in\mathrm{CN}(u,v)}\frac1{\ln k_w}\]</div><p class="formula-where">A common neighbour has \(k_w\ge2\), so \(\ln k_w&gt;0\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Resource allocation (Zhou, Lu and Zhang 2009)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{RA}(u,v)=\sum_{w\in\mathrm{CN}(u,v)}\frac1{k_w}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Each common neighbour passes on a unit of resource shared equally among its neighbours.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Preferential attachment (Barabasi and Albert 1999)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{PA}(u,v)=k_u\,k_v\]</div><p class="formula-where">Product of the degrees.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Common neighbours with communities (Soundarajan and Hopcroft 2012)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{SH}(u,v)=|\mathrm{CN}|+\bigl|\{w\in\mathrm{CN}:\ c(w)=c(u)=c(v)\}\bigr|;\qquad s_{WI}(u,v)=\frac{|\{w\in\mathrm{CN}:\ c(w)=c(u)=c(v)\}|+\delta}{|\{w\in\mathrm{CN}:\ c(w)\ne c(u)\text{ or }c(w)\ne c(v)\}|+\delta}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Common neighbours in the same community as both nodes count extra (\(s_{SH}\)), or as a ratio to the others (\(s_{WI}\), within versus inter-cluster).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Common neighbour and centrality (Ahmad et al. 2020)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[s_{CCPA}(u,v)=\alpha\,|\mathrm{CN}|+(1-\alpha)\frac{n}{d(u,v)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mixes the number of common neighbours with the inverse shortest-path distance; needs \(u,v\) connected (\(d&lt;\infty\)).</p></div>
