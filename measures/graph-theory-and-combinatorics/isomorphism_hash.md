# Weisfeiler-Lehman graph and subgraph hash {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Isomorphism and subgraph matching. Concept id: `isomorphism_hash`. 2 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Weisfeiler-Lehman graph and subgraph hash. Entries grouped here: Wl hash; Wl subgraph.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Weisfeiler-Lehman relabelling (Shervashidze et al. 2011)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[h_0(v)=\text{initial label (degree or attribute)},\qquad h_{t+1}(v)=H\bigl(h_t(v),\ \mathrm{sort}\{h_t(u):u\in N(v)\}\bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Each round replaces a node's label by a hash of its own label and the sorted labels of its neighbours (edge labels can be included).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Graph hash</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\mathrm{WL}(G)=H\bigl(\ \mathrm{multiset}\{h_T(v)\}_{v}\ \text{(over all rounds)}\bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Isomorphic graphs have equal hashes; equal hashes do not imply isomorphism (1-WL cannot separate, for example, regular graphs of the same degree and order). Subgraph hashes keep \(h_t(v)\) per node and round.</p></div>
