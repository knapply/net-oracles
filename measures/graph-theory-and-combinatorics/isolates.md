# Isolates {#concept-top}

Family: [Graph theory and combinatorics](index.html). Subfamily: Degree sequences and predicates. Concept id: `isolates`. 2 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Isolates. Entries grouped here: Is isolate; Isolates.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Isolated node</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[v\ \text{isolated}\iff d_v=0\]</div><p class="formula-where">No edge is incident to \(v\). A self-loop contributes to the degree of its node (by 1 or 2 depending on the loop convention), so a node whose only edge is a loop has \(d_v&gt;0\) and is not isolated by this formula, while the vocabulary reading 'no edge to another node' would call it isolated.</p></div>
