## Definition {#concept-definition}

Matching and perfect-matching tests. Entries grouped here: Check matching.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Matching, maximal and perfect matching (Bondy and Murty 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\text{matching: }e\cap f=\emptyset\ \forall e\ne f\in M;\quad\text{maximal: no }e\in E\setminus M\text{ with }M\cup\{e\}\text{ a matching};\quad\text{perfect: every node is covered by }M\]</div><p class="formula-where">Maximal means not extendable; maximum means largest. A perfect matching has \(n/2\) edges and needs \(n\) even.</p></div>
