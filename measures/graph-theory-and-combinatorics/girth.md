## Definition {#concept-definition}

Girth. Entries grouped here: Girth.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Girth (Bondy and Murty 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[g(G)=\min_{C\ \text{cycle}}\ell(C),\qquad g(G)=\infty\ \text{if }G\text{ is acyclic}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The length of a shortest cycle; \(\infty\) for a forest. In a multigraph a loop gives 1 and parallel edges 2. For a digraph the shortest directed cycle.</p></div>
