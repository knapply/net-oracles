# Local bridges {#concept-top}

Family: [Components and connectivity](index.html). Subfamily: Articulation points, bridges and biconnected components. Concept id: `local_bridges`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\), \(n=|V|\), \(m=|E|\); every edge has a source and a target (the ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(N(u)\)</td><td>neighbours of \(u\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(\mathrm{span}(u,v)\)</td><td>distance between \(u\) and \(v\) after the edge \(uv\) is removed from \(G\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Local bridges. Entries grouped here: Local bridges.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Local bridge (Granovetter 1973)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[\{u,v\}\text{ is a local bridge}\iff N(u)\cap N(v)=\emptyset\ (\mathrm{span}(u,v)&gt;2)\]</div><p class="formula-where">An edge whose ends have no common neighbour; deleting it increases the distance between its ends to more than 2 (infinite for a bridge, which is the extreme case). The span is the distance after deletion.</p></div>
