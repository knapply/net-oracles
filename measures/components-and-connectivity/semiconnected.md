## Definition {#concept-definition}

Semiconnected graphs. Entries grouped here: Semiconnected; Is semi connected.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Semiconnected (unilaterally connected) digraph (Harary, Norman and Cartwright 1965)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[\forall u,v\in V:\ u\to^*v\ \text{ or }\ v\to^*u\]</div><p class="formula-where">Every pair of nodes is joined by a directed path in at least one direction; strongly connected implies semiconnected implies weakly connected. Equivalent to the condensation having a Hamiltonian path.</p></div>
