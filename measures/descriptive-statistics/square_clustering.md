Family: [Descriptive statistics](index.html). Subfamily: Clustering and transitivity. Concept id: `square_clustering`. 2 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Square clustering of every node: the share of possible squares through it that exist.

*Definition partly written:* cat\_clustering\_coefficient\_local\_square (NetworKit's local square clustering) is documented only and was never run; its definition is not recorded in the sources.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Square clustering (Lind, Gonzalez and Herrmann 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_4(v)=\frac{\sum_{u&lt;w}q_v(u,w)}{\sum_{u&lt;w}\bigl[a_v(u,w)+q_v(u,w)\bigr]}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Fraction of possible four-cycles through \(v\) that exist; \(u,w\) range over distinct neighbours of \(v\). Defined when the denominator is positive; 0/0 for nodes with fewer than two neighbours. Zhang et al. 2008 and Zlatic et al. 2012 use the same cycle-closing idea for bipartite and multi-edge networks.</p></div>
