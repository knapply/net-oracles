## Definition {#concept-definition}

Mean local clustering coefficient over the nodes.

*Definition partly written:* cat\_clustering\_extended (graph-tool's extended clustering) is documented only and was never run; its definition is not recorded in the sources.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Local clustering coefficient (Watts and Strogatz 1998)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_i=\frac{2\,t_i}{k_i(k_i-1)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Fraction of pairs of distinct neighbours of \(i\) that are adjacent. Defined for \(k_i\ge2\); the denominator is 0 below two neighbours.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Average clustering</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[\bar C=\frac1{|V'|}\sum_{i\in V'}C_i\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean of \(C_i\); the node set \(V'\) is either the nodes with \(k_i\ge2\) (where \(C_i\) is defined) or all of \(V\) with \(C_i:=0\) below two neighbours. These give different values when low-degree nodes exist.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Weighted clustering (Barrat et al. 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_i^{w}=\frac{1}{s_i(k_i-1)}\sum_{j,h}\frac{w_{ij}+w_{ih}}{2}\,a_{ij}a_{ih}a_{jh}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Triangles weighted by the average of the two weights at \(i\); defined for \(k_i\ge2\) and \(s_i&gt;0\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Weighted clustering (Onnela et al. 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[\tilde C_i=\frac{1}{k_i(k_i-1)}\sum_{j,h}\bigl(\hat w_{ij}\hat w_{ih}\hat w_{jh}\bigr)^{1/3}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Geometric mean of the three scaled triangle weights, summed over ordered neighbour pairs; defined for \(k_i\ge2\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Directed clustering (Fagiolo 2007)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_i^{D}=\frac{\bigl[(A+A^{\mathsf T})^3\bigr]_{ii}}{2\bigl[d_i^{\mathrm{tot}}(d_i^{\mathrm{tot}}-1)-2d_i^{\leftrightarrow}\bigr]}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Counts all directed triangle types through \(i\) relative to the possible ones; the denominator is 0 when \(i\) has fewer than two distinct partners.</p></div>
