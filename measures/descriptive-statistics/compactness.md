# Compactness {#concept-top}

Family: [Descriptive statistics](index.html). Subfamily: Efficiency, compactness and hierarchy. Concept id: `compactness`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(d(i,j)\)</td><td>length of a shortest path from \(i\) to \(j\) (edge count, or the total of the edge lengths when weighted); \(\infty\) when unreachable</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Compactness. Entries grouped here: Compactness.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Compactness (as efficiency of Latora and Marchiori 2001)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\mathrm{Comp}=\frac{1}{n(n-1)}\sum_{i\ne j}\frac{1}{d(i,j)}\qquad\Bigl(\tfrac1\infty=0\Bigr)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean of the reciprocal distances over ordered pairs; unreachable pairs contribute 0, so it is defined on disconnected graphs; it is 1 for a complete graph. Defined for \(n\ge2\). The same quantity is the global efficiency of Latora and Marchiori 2001.</p></div>
