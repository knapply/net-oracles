# Reciprocity {#concept-top}

Family: [Descriptive statistics](index.html). Subfamily: Reciprocity, dyad and triad census. Concept id: `reciprocity`. 2 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Reciprocity. Entries grouped here: Reciprocity; Node reciprocity.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Arc reciprocity (ratio of reciprocated arcs)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[r=\frac{L^{\leftrightarrow}}{L}=\frac{2M}{2M+A}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Share of arcs that are reciprocated. Defined when \(L&gt;0\); 0/0 without arcs.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Dyadic reciprocity</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[r_{\mathrm{dyad}}=\frac{M+N}{\binom n2}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Share of dyads in which both nodes agree (both arcs or none); defined for \(n\ge2\).</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Dyad-based ratio</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[r_{\mathrm{ratio}}=\frac{M}{M+A}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mutual dyads over non-null dyads; defined when some tie exists.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Reciprocity correlation (Garlaschelli and Loffredo 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[\rho=\frac{\sum_{i\ne j}(x_{ij}-\bar x)(x_{ji}-\bar x)}{\sum_{i\ne j}(x_{ij}-\bar x)^2}=\frac{r-\bar x}{1-\bar x}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Correlation between the two directions of ordered pairs; positive for more reciprocity than chance. Defined when \(0&lt;\bar x&lt;1\); 0/0 for an empty or complete graph.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Node reciprocity</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[r(v)=\frac{|\mathrm{in}(v)\cap\mathrm{out}(v)|\cdot 2}{|\mathrm{in}(v)|+|\mathrm{out}(v)|}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Share of the arcs at \(v\) that are reciprocated; defined when \(v\) has at least one arc.</p></div>
