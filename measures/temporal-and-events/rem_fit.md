## Definition {#concept-definition}

Relational event model fit. Entries grouped here: Rem fit.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Ordinal relational event likelihood (Butts 2008; Perry and Wolfe 2013)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any, directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[L(\theta)=\prod_{e}\frac{\exp\{\theta^{\top}x(s_e,r_e,t_e)\}}{\sum_{(s,r)\in R_{t_e}}\exp\{\theta^{\top}x(s,r,t_e)\}}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The partial likelihood of the observed order of events: at each event the observed dyad competes with every dyad of the risk set. Only the order of events enters, not the time gaps.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Score and Newton step</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\nabla\ell(\theta)=\sum_e\Bigl(x_e-\mathbb E_{\theta,e}[x]\Bigr),\qquad \theta\leftarrow\theta+\mathcal I(\theta)^{-1}\nabla\ell(\theta),\quad \mathcal I(\theta)=\sum_e\operatorname{Cov}_{\theta,e}(x)\]</div><p class="formula-where">The log partial likelihood \(\ell=\log L\) is concave in \(\theta\); \(\mathbb E_{\theta,e}\) and \(\operatorname{Cov}_{\theta,e}\) are the mean and covariance of \(x\) over \(R_{t_e}\) with probabilities \(\propto e^{\theta^{\top}x}\). Standard errors are the square roots of the diagonal of \(\mathcal I(\hat\theta)^{-1}\). A finite maximizer exists only if the likelihood is not monotone.</p></div>
