# Average neighbor degree and degree connectivity {#concept-top}

Family: [Mixing and assortativity](index.html). Subfamily: Assortativity. Concept id: `average_neighbor_degree`. 3 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Average neighbor degree and degree connectivity. Entries grouped here: Avg deg conn; Avg nbr deg; Knn.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Average nearest-neighbour degree (Pastor-Satorras, Vazquez and Vespignani 2001)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[k_{nn,i}=\frac{1}{k_i}\sum_{j}a_{ij}k_j\]</div><p class="formula-where">Mean degree of the neighbours of \(i\), each edge counted once. Defined for \(k_i&gt;0\); for an isolate the sum is empty and the divisor is 0, so not defined.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Degree connectivity</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[k_{nn}(k)=\frac{1}{|V_k|}\sum_{i\in V_k}k_{nn,i}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Average of the nearest-neighbour degree over all nodes of degree \(k\); an increasing function signals assortative mixing.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Weighted average nearest-neighbour degree (Barrat et al. 2004)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: weighted</span></p><div class="formula">\[k^{w}_{nn,i}=\frac{1}{s_i}\sum_j w_{ij}k_j\]</div><p class="formula-where">Neighbour degrees weighted by tie weight and divided by the strength; defined for \(s_i&gt;0\).</p></div>
