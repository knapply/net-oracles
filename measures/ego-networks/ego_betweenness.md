# Ego betweenness {#concept-top}

Family: [Ego networks](index.html). Subfamily: Ego measures. Concept id: `ego_betweenness`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>simple graph (parallel edges merged, loops dropped) with node set \(V\); each edge has a source and a target</td></tr><tr><td>\(v\)</td><td>the ego, a node of \(V\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(A\)</td><td>adjacency matrix of the ego network including the ego (symmetric, simple)</td></tr><tr><td>\(N(v)\)</td><td>neighbours of \(v\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(g_{jk}\)</td><td>number of shortest paths between \(j,k\) in the ego network; for non-adjacent alters \(g_{jk}=(A^2)_{jk}\)</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Ego betweenness. Entries grouped here: Ego\_summary / betweenness.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Ego betweenness (Everett and Borgatti 2005)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected, simple</span></p><div class="formula">\[C_E(v)=\sum_{\substack{\{j,k\}\subseteq N(v)\\ a_{jk}=0}}\frac{1}{(A^2)_{jk}}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Betweenness of the ego within its own ego network: for each non-adjacent pair of alters the ego lies on the share \(1/g_{jk}\) of the shortest paths, and all such paths have length 2. Equals the usual betweenness of \(v\) in \(G[N(v)\cup\{v\}]\), raw (unnormalized).</p></div>

## Variants {#concept-variants}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="variants"><thead><tr><th>Variant</th><th>Measure id</th><th>Tools</th><th>What changes</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><a href="#ego_summary-betweenness">Ego_summary / betweenness</a></td><td><code>ego_summary:betweenness</code></td><td>3</td><td>Betweenness of the ego in the subgraph induced by the ego and its alters within k steps: k=1 undirected (ego_betweenness), k=1 over successors with directed paths (ego_betweenness_out), k=2 (ego_betweenness_order2, ordinary shortest-path betweenness of the ego in a larger induced subgraph, no longer the matrix form).</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
