# Ego network ties {#concept-top}

Family: [Ego networks](index.html). Subfamily: Ego measures. Concept id: `ego_ties`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>simple graph (parallel edges merged, loops dropped) with node set \(V\); each edge has a source and a target</td></tr><tr><td>\(v\)</td><td>the ego, a node of \(V\)</td></tr><tr><td>\(N_k(v)\)</td><td>alters of \(v\) within \(k\) steps: \(\{u\ne v: d(v,u)\le k\}\); for a directed graph the steps follow edge directions out of \(v\) (out version), into \(v\) (in version) or ignore direction</td></tr><tr><td>\(G_k(v)\)</td><td>ego network: the subgraph induced by \(N_k(v)\) (alters only) or by \(N_k(v)\cup\{v\}\) (ego included), as stated in each formula</td></tr><tr><td>\(a_{ij}\)</td><td>1 if \(i\) and \(j\) are joined by an edge (from \(i\) to \(j\) for directed graphs), else 0</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Ego network ties. Entries grouped here: Ego\_summary / ties.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Ties among alters</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[t_k(v)=\sum_{\{i,j\}\subseteq N_k(v)}a_{ij}\;\text{(undirected)},\qquad t_k(v)=\sum_{i\ne j\in N_k(v)}a_{ij}\;\text{(directed)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Number of distinct ties in the ego network without the ego, counted on the simple graph: each pair once (undirected) or each ordered pair once (directed). Ties from the ego to alters are excluded.</p></div>
