# Breadth-first search {#concept-top}

Family: [Traversal, trees and DAGs](index.html). Subfamily: Traversal and orderings. Concept id: `breadth_first_search`. 8 measures.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Levels, parents and counts of a breadth-first search from one node (direction-optimizing).

*Definition partly written:* Three documented-only entries (cat\_bfs\_iterator, cat\_bfs\_random, cat\_bfs\_reverse) are described from their names; the exact output of the beam search depends on its score function and width.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Breadth-first search (Moore 1959)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[L_0=\{s\},\quad L_{k+1}=\{v\notin L_0\cup\cdots\cup L_k:\ \exists u\in L_k,\ (u,v)\in E\}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Nodes are visited layer by layer, so each is visited at its hop distance \(d(s,v)\) from \(s\); the BFS tree edges \((\pi(v),v)\) form a shortest-path tree. Layers are determined by the graph; the order inside a layer and the parents are not.</p></div>
