## Definition {#concept-definition}

Random walks. Entries grouped here: Random walk generation and sampling; Random walks.

*Definition partly written:* The documented tool entries are not specified beyond their names (walk length, restart rule, sampling scheme not recorded).

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Simple random walk (Kemeny and Snell 1960)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Pr(X_{t+1}=v\mid X_t=u)=\frac{w(u,v)}{\sum_{x}w(u,x)}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Move along an out-edge chosen in proportion to its weight (uniformly if unweighted); not defined at a node without out-edges.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Biased second-order walk (Grover and Leskovec 2016)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: any</span></p><div class="formula">\[\Pr(X_{t+1}=x\mid X_t=v,X_{t-1}=u)\propto\alpha_{pq}(u,x)\,w(v,x),\quad \alpha_{pq}=\begin{cases}1/p&amp;d_{ux}=0\\1&amp;d_{ux}=1\\1/q&amp;d_{ux}=2\end{cases}\]</div><p class="formula-where">Return parameter \(p\) and in-out parameter \(q\) bias the step by the distance \(d_{ux}\) of the candidate from the previous node; \(p=q=1\) is the simple walk.</p></div>
