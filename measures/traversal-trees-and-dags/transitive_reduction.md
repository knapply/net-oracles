# Transitive reduction {#concept-top}

Family: [Traversal, trees and DAGs](index.html). Subfamily: DAGs and topological order. Concept id: `transitive_reduction`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

The arcs of the transitive reduction of a DAG, sorted by source then target.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Transitive reduction (Aho, Garey and Ullman 1972)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[E^{-}=\{(u,v)\in E:\ \text{no path }u\leadsto v\text{ of length}\ge2\}\]</div><p class="formula-where">The fewest arcs with the same reachability as \(G\); unique for a DAG. For a graph with cycles a minimal graph is not unique, and the definition's formula would drop every arc of a strongly connected component that also has a longer path.</p></div>
