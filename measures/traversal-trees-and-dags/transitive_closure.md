# Transitive closure {#concept-top}

Family: [Traversal, trees and DAGs](index.html). Subfamily: DAGs and topological order. Concept id: `transitive_closure`. 1 measure.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

<div class="table-wrapper"><table class="notation"><thead><tr><th>Symbol</th><th>Meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>\(G=(V,E)\)</td><td>graph with node set \(V\) and edge set \(E\); every edge has a source and a target (the two ends of an undirected edge are interchangeable)</td></tr><tr><td>\(n=|V|\)</td><td>number of nodes</td></tr><tr><td>\(m=|E|\)</td><td>number of edges</td></tr><tr><td>\(E^{+}\)</td><td>transitive closure of the arc set</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Definition {#concept-definition}

The pairs (u, v) of the transitive closure, sorted by source then target.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Transitive closure (Warshall 1962)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[(u,v)\in E^{+}\iff u\leadsto v\]</div><p class="formula-where">The least transitive relation containing \(E\): all pairs joined by a directed path of at least one arc. The reflexive form also contains \((u,u)\) for every node.</p></div>
