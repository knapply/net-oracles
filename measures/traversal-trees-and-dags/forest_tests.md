## Definition {#concept-definition}

Tree, forest and arborescence tests. Entries grouped here: Forest flags.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Tree and forest (Bondy and Murty 2008)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[\text{forest}\iff\text{no cycle}\iff m=n-c;\qquad \text{tree}\iff\text{forest and }c=1\]</div><p class="formula-where">A cycle includes a loop and a pair of parallel edges.</p></div>

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Branching and arborescence (Edmonds 1967)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[\text{branching}\iff\text{forest (ignoring direction) and }d^{\text{in}}(v)\le1\ \forall v;\qquad \text{arborescence}\iff\text{branching and }c=1\]</div><p class="formula-where">An arborescence has exactly one node of in-degree 0 (the root) from which every node is reachable.</p></div>
