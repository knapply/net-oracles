## Definition {#concept-definition}

DAG test. Entries grouped here: Is dag.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Acyclicity (Kahn 1962)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[G\ \text{is a DAG}\iff\text{no directed cycle}\iff\exists\ \text{a topological order of }V\]</div><p class="formula-where">Kahn's procedure removes nodes without incoming arcs repeatedly; \(G\) is a DAG exactly when all nodes are removed. A self-loop is a cycle of length 1.</p></div>
