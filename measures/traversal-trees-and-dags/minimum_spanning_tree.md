# Minimum spanning tree {#concept-top}

Family: [Traversal, trees and DAGs](index.html). Subfamily: Spanning trees and branchings. Concept id: `minimum_spanning_tree`. 3 measures.

## Notation {#concept-notation}

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Minimum or maximum spanning forest of an undirected network, as input edge positions and total weight.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Minimum spanning forest (Boruvka 1926; Kruskal 1956; Prim 1957)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: undirected</span></p><div class="formula">\[T^{*}=\arg\min_{T\ \text{spanning forest}}\sum_{e\in T}w(e),\qquad W(T^{*})=\sum_{e\in T^{*}}w(e)\]</div><p class="formula-where">The tree edges have \(n-c\) edges for \(c\) components. The weight \(W\) is unique; the tree is unique when the weights are distinct. The maximum spanning forest is the same with \(\max\), or with \(-w\).</p></div>
