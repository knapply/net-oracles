Family: [Traversal, trees and DAGs](index.html). Subfamily: DAGs and topological order. Concept id: `topological_sort`. 1 measure.

## Definition {#concept-definition}

Topological sort. Entries grouped here: Topological sort.

<div class="formula-block"><p class="formula-name"><strong>Topological order (Kahn 1962; Knuth 1974)</strong> <span class="applies">applies to: directed</span></p><div class="formula">\[\sigma(u)&lt;\sigma(v)\ \ \text{for every arc }(u,v)\]</div><p class="formula-where">Any linear extension of the reachability order; it exists iff the graph is acyclic and is unique iff the DAG has a Hamiltonian path.</p></div>
